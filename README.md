# Where Prussia Fought

An interactive map, the Prussian win-draw-loss record and the full list of the 584 named land and sea battles fought by
Brandenburg-Prussia, from the Thirty Years' War (1618) to the founding of the German Empire (1871): Brandenburg-Prussia under the
electors, the Kingdom of Prussia and the North German Confederation. Each battle records the Prussian state and role, the Prussian force,
strengths and losses, every doubt and flag, and its sources.

**Open the page:** https://iosiflazaridis.github.io/battles-of-prussia/

- `index.html`: the page (self-contained; the data are embedded)
- `img/`: the 10 images in full size (3200 × 1800); `thumb/`: their thumbnails
- `dl/`: the data as CSV, JSON and an Excel workbook (with the record tables and the entries left out, with reasons)

Record: 304 won, 26 drawn, 174 lost in the 504 battles fought as principal or major ally with a known result;
80 more are listed but not scored (61 of small Prussian contingents, 16 with unknown results, 3 civil).

Sources: cited battle by battle in the data. Map: Natural Earth.
